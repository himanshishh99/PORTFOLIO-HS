Small poems for distances, grief-spaces, last leaves, and the stubborn hope that people who are meant to find each other still do.

Parallel lovers

The horizon and the sea There is something between us There is also nothing between us We reach out to each other (Or atleast try to) My hands try to grasp your fingertips hanging in the air, I get so close yet the distance forever bares. I call out to you, and perhaps you hear it too Yet the echoes of your response gets lost through. To those with sight, you and I, my love Are surely a sight. I caress you along the shore line, Like a melody that’s supposedly mine. I hunt for you, day and night, If only for the brief moments where I appear to embrace you for “auld lang syne”. Perhaps in some universe my beloved, we may collide.

The space people leave behind

There’s a hollow space people leave behind, much like a gigantic tree stolen from a thicket so uptight, that on that hollow land now only grief suffices. It’s suffocating; all that open space; which is ironic considering breathing liberated me so often and now that empty land chases me off to a land I can’t yet call forgotten. I hold my breath when I so much so even look at the space left behind; hoping it takes away the wounds I can’t hide. It shouldn’t be so hard, considering it was snatched from me upright, yet the bloodstains still cover the canopy left behind. {waiting, holding space, with hope above all} Shouldn’t have been that hard; considering, even the rain bent down and whispered it’s failed attempts at washing away the smeared darkness I hold so profound.

When the last leaf falls

When the last river dries When the last child cries When the last tear fades When the facade truly breaks I hope I get to hold your hand, I hope I get to listen to your heartbeat, I hope we are at peace now if never before been, I hope I get to see you smile I hope I get to tell you; living with you was worth awhile. Of all I hope, In another scenerio if a strong willed sapling finds a way to grow through what I assume are the rotten remains Of you and I I hope you and I Find each other.
